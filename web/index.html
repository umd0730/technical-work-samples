GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions.

Is this a real workshop?

No. This is an original, self-initiated website demo. It is not a past client engagement, and no place, booking or payment is offered.

What was repaired in this example?

The intentionally broken version uses a fixed-width layout. The repaired page reflows into one column on small screens, keeps long text inside its container and provides a keyboard-operable menu.

Does the enquiry form send anything?

No. It validates fields locally and shows a preview message. Nothing is sent or saved. Use made-up details to try it.
